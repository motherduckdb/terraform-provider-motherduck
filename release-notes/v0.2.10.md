## Highlights

- Audit share audiences against an explicit allowlist and optionally fail Terraform plans when missing or unexpected grants persist. [#48](https://github.com/motherduckdb/terraform-provider-motherduck/pull/48)

## Features

- Inspect direct user and role grants, plus organization-wide or public audiences, with the `motherduck_share_grants` data source. It does not expand role membership or change permissions. [#42](https://github.com/motherduckdb/terraform-provider-motherduck/pull/42)

## Maintenance

- Follow a runnable team-role and share-access example, with guidance for importing grants and auditing access outside Terraform state. [#43](https://github.com/motherduckdb/terraform-provider-motherduck/pull/43) [#47](https://github.com/motherduckdb/terraform-provider-motherduck/pull/47)
- Simplify deterministic SQL argument ordering and reuse Go's HTTP request-body replay while preserving existing output and retry behavior. [#44](https://github.com/motherduckdb/terraform-provider-motherduck/pull/44) [#46](https://github.com/motherduckdb/terraform-provider-motherduck/pull/46)
